Dissection and integration of bursty transcriptional dynamics for complex systems

PNAS · 2024

First-author (Only non-senior) work on a novel RNA velocity method leveraging topic modeling and chemical master equations to infer cell-state transitions from scRNA-seq data. This method discovers experimentally validated transitions in complex immune systems and nominates key genes driving inflammation responses.
