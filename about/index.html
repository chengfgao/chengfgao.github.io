Cheng “Frank” Gao 高丞

My name is Cheng “Frank” Gao (高丞).

I am a research associate (member of investment committee) at Dimension, a VC firm that invests across life sciences and technology. I support our portfolio companies, incubation efforts, due diligence process and help orchestrate software infrastructure.

I completed my PhD in chemistry at the University of Chicago, jointly advised by Prof. Suri Vaikuntanathan and Prof. Samantha Riesenfeld. My PhD work focuses on developing physics-informed machine learning tools to decipher dynamics from single-cell RNA sequencing and spatial transcriptomics data. I have also carried out research in predicting protein-protein interactions and optimizing protein design. I was a Seymour Goodman fellow and Polsky Commercialization Fellow for the 24-25 academic year. I was an Eckhardt Fellow from 2019 to 2024. Before UChicago, I completed my undergrad at the College of Chemistry within UC Berkeley with a degree in chemistry (High Honors) where I was intensively trained as a synthetic biologist and as a theoretical chemist (with a focus in statistical mechanics and molecular dynamics simulation particularly for transport phenomenons).

Outside of academic research, I improved my machine learning skills for translational researches as a DSAI (Data Science & Artificial Intelligence) co-op at Moderna and ML intern at AbbVie, sharpened my investment senses as an analyst intern at ARCH venture partners and as an Bio-IT fellow at 8VC. I was also fortunate enough to be trained as an entrepreneurial scientist as an AI/ML fellow at Flagship Pioneering and as an member of the U.S. c6 of the 5050 program by Fifty Years. These experiences have significantly broadened my scientific training and understanding of the biotech industry.
