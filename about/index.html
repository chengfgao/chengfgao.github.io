Cheng “Frank” Gao 高丞

Cheng “Frank” Gao (高丞) is a computational scientist with research experience in machine learning, statistical mechanics, and bioinformatics. He is passionate about integrating advancements in artificial intelligence with fundamental biological sciences to discover more effective medicines and extend healthy lifespans.

Frank received his BS in Chemistry from UC Berkeley with high honors. He then earned his MS and PhD in Chemistry from the University of Chicago, where he was an Eckhardt Scholar, and received awards and fellowships across teaching, research, and commercialization. His doctoral research spanned developing novel physics-informed algorithms for inferring cellular responses and representation learning for deciphering protein-protein interactions; his lead-author works have been published in top peer-reviewed journals.

During his PhD, Frank gained industry experience as a machine learning intern at Moderna and AbbVie. He was also an AI/ML Fellow at Flagship Pioneering, an intern at ARCH Venture Partners, a Bio-IT Fellow at 8VC, and a cohort member of 5050 US C6.
